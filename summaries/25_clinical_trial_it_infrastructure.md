This chapter answers which computerized systems a clinical trial runs on and what makes them defensible in an inspection. It describes the four core platforms, EDC (electronic data capture), CTMS (clinical trial management system), eTMF (electronic trial master file) and RTSM (randomization and trial supply management, also called interactive response technology), and how they connect in single vendor and mixed vendor stacks. It compares major vendors (Medidata, Veeva, Oracle, IQVIA), eTMF platforms with artificial intelligence (AI) features such as auto indexing and completeness prediction, and decentralized trial tools from Medable. The regulatory section separates enforceable law from guidance and sets out validation, audit trail and access control expectations under U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) 21 CFR Part 11, European Medicines Agency (EMA) guidance, EU GMP Annex 11 and International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) E6(R3). A reader can place each system in the trial workflow and list what an inspection tests.
