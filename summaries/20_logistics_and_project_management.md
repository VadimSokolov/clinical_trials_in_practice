How are the people, documents, supplies and processes of a clinical trial coordinated so that a protocol becomes executed research? The chapter covers the roles of sponsor, principal investigator, contract research organization (CRO) and study coordinator, delegation and accountability under 21 CFR Part 312 and U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Form 1572, partner selection by request for proposal, and biospecimen chain of custody. It relates the protocol to standard operating procedures (SOPs), and describes the Trial Master File (TMF) and Investigator Site File. It gives development timelines and success rates, multiregional clinical trials under International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) guidelines E5 and E17, startup bottlenecks, and project management by scope, schedule, budget, quality and critical path. It also treats direct to patient and decentralized trials, Good Publication Practice (GPP 2022), and an ancillary study policy aligned with the National Institutes of Health (NIH) Policy for Data Management and Sharing. A reader can set delegation and oversight, plan timelines and budgets, and choose an execution model.
