What makes clinical research ethical, beyond informed consent? The chapter presents the seven requirements of Emanuel, Wendler and Grady (value, scientific validity, fair subject selection, favorable risk benefit ratio, independent review, informed consent, respect for participants), then shows where they collide in practice: placebo controls under the Declaration of Helsinki, clinical equipoise, vulnerable populations under the Common Rule (45 CFR 46), and research in resource limited settings. It takes up data privacy in decentralized trials, electronic informed consent (eConsent) under Food and Drug Administration (FDA) guidance, and AI and automation, covering bias auditing, transparency in consent and accountability. A closing section describes institutional review board (IRB) review: initial, continuing (at least annually), modification, adverse event and final review, and the decisive authority of the IRB. A reader can test a design, a population choice or an AI use against the seven requirements and plan the safeguards and IRB reviews.
