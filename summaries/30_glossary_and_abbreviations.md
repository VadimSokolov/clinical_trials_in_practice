This appendix is the book's reference list of abbreviations and of statistical and clinical trial terms. The abbreviations table gives each term in full with a short definition, listed alphabetically and covering regulators and agencies (FDA, EMA, PMDA, NMPA), regulatory submissions (NDA, BLA, MAA, CTD, eCTD), good practice and quality (GCP, GMP, GLP, GAMP, ALCOA), data standards and systems (CDISC, SDTM, ADaM, EDC, CTMS, IRT), safety reporting and monitoring (SAE, ADR, SUSAR, DSMB) and artificial intelligence (LLM, RAG, MCP). The statistical terms table defines alpha, power, Type I and Type II error, p-value, confidence interval, estimand, intercurrent events, hazard ratio, Bayesian and frequentist approaches, and master protocol designs. Most entries carry no citation. A few name their source: International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) E2A for adverse drug reaction and serious adverse event, ICH E6 for Good Clinical Practice (GCP) and the trial master file, and 21 CFR 312.40 for the Investigational New Drug (IND) application. A reader can look up an abbreviation or term as the book defines it.
