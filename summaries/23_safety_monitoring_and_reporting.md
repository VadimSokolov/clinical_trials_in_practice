This chapter answers how adverse events in a clinical trial are classified, reported and interpreted, and which reporting clocks apply. It defines adverse event (AE), serious adverse event (SAE), adverse drug reaction (ADR), suspected unexpected serious adverse reaction (SUSAR) and adverse event of special interest (AESI), and separates seriousness from severity, most often graded on the CTCAE scale (Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events). It sets out investigator and sponsor duties: immediate SAE reporting, expedited reports to regulators within 7 or 15 calendar days, safety reports to investigators, and the annual Development Safety Update Report (DSUR). It covers reconciliation of safety and clinical databases, laboratory safety analysis, signal detection, reporting rules of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the European Union, which rest on International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) E2A and E2B, and benefit risk assessment. A reader can classify an event, find its reporting deadline and choose analyses for finding a safety signal.
