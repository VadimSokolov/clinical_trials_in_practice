Which rules govern a clinical trial in the United States and internationally? The chapter covers the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER), Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER) and Center for Devices and Radiological Health (CDRH); the Code of Federal Regulations parts for trials (21 CFR Parts 11, 50, 56 and 312); the Investigational New Drug (IND) application, with its 30 day review and clinical hold; and the duties of sponsor, contract research organization (CRO), investigator and institutional review board (IRB). It then covers International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) guidelines (E6 Good Clinical Practice, E8, E9, M4), the EU Clinical Trials Regulation, essential documents and the Trial Master File, FDA inspection outcomes from no action indicated to official action indicated, and the governance of AI and automation in regulated trials. A further section gives regulatory expectations for adaptive and Bayesian designs, including the 2019 FDA guidance, ICH E20 and simulation as evidence. A reader can decide which center and IND route apply, how long to keep records, and what to agree with FDA before enrolling.
