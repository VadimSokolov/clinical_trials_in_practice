Why do trials struggle to recruit and retain participants, and what can sponsors and sites do about it? The chapter treats recruitment as an attrition problem, with an illustrative funnel, and compares provider referral, community outreach, direct advertising, social media, registries and decentralized elements. It covers barriers, geographic access for rural patients, retention, investigator and coordinator attrition, representative enrollment, and metrics such as screen failure rate and enrollment rate. A large section treats AI and machine learning enabled recruitment: identification from electronic health records (EHRs) with computable phenotypes, Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics (OHDSI) and its OMOP Common Data Model, SMART on FHIR, conversational agents, electronic informed consent, and the SPIRIT-AI, CONSORT-AI and TRIPOD+AI reporting extensions. A reader can plan recruitment channels, set tracking metrics, and judge the risks of AI tools that decide who is contacted.
