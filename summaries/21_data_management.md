This chapter explains how observations made at a trial site become data that a regulator can trust and trace back to source. It follows the clinical data management pipeline: electronic data capture (EDC), edit checks, audit trails, queries, source documents, eSource, source data verification (SDV) and database lock. It covers data arriving outside EDC (central laboratories, imaging, wearables, real world data) and a data transfer specification checklist. It presents common data elements, the ALCOA, ALCOA+ and ALCOA++ integrity principles, Clinical Data Interchange Standards Consortium (CDISC) standards, and the traceability chain to the electronic Common Technical Document (eCTD). Compliance topics are 21 CFR Part 11, computer system validation, and records retention under U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and European Union rules. A reader can plan data capture and cleaning, specify external transfers, and test whether a submitted number traces to source.
