This chapter explains what Phase II must establish: whether a drug produces a worthwhile therapeutic effect, and at what dose it goes into Phase III. About 70% of drugs entering Phase II do not advance to Phase III. It separates Phase IIa proof of concept from Phase IIb dose finding, and covers dose response, the Emax model, MCP-Mod and Bayesian adaptive dose finding, surrogate endpoints, the International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) E9(R1) estimands framework, clinical outcome assessments and biomarkers. It also covers adaptive designs, futility stopping by conditional power and predictive probability of success (PPoS), and the go or no go decision. A reader can choose a dose finding approach, state an estimand for a surrogate endpoint, prespecify a futility rule and check it by simulation, and weigh efficacy, safety, dose and commercial viability before Phase III.
