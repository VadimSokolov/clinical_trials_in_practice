This chapter asks how a sponsor keeps a running trial safe, competent and compliant with Good Clinical Practice (GCP), and how that oversight is checked from outside. It describes the clinical research associate (CRA), the four visit types (qualification, initiation, interim monitoring, closeout), source data verification (SDV), source document review and the monitoring report. It presents risk based quality management (RBQM): risk assessment, quality tolerance limits, key risk indicators, and centralized, remote and hybrid monitoring. It separates quality control, quality assurance, sponsor audits and regulatory inspections, with an inspection readiness checklist, Form 483 from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and corrective and preventive action (CAPA). It closes with governance committees: data safety monitoring boards (DSMB), including platform trials, endpoint adjudication committees and steering committees. A reader can draft a risk based monitoring plan and prepare for inspection.
