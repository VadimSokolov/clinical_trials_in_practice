This chapter explains what is studied and monitored after approval, and which of those studies a sponsor can be compelled to complete. It classifies Phase IV studies (post marketing safety and efficacy studies, requirements and commitments, label extensions, outcomes research) and describes how FDORA (Food and Drug Omnibus Reform Act, 2022) lets the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) require a confirmatory trial to be underway before, or within a set period after, accelerated approval. It covers pharmacovigilance through spontaneous reporting (FAERS and AEMS, its replacement launched in March 2026) and active surveillance (Sentinel System; DARWIN EU, the Data Analysis and Real World Interrogation Network), Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategies (REMS), comparative effectiveness research, patient registries, real world evidence, health technology assessment and lifecycle management. A reader can tell required from voluntary studies, see where a spontaneous report signal is confirmed, and identify the evidence payers ask for.
