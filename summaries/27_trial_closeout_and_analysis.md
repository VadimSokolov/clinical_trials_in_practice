This chapter covers the period after the last patient last visit (LPLV) and up to the end of a trial, and how to interpret the result. It sets out the closeout sequence: query resolution, medical coding, database lock, a statistical analysis plan (SAP) finalized before unblinding, and the analysis that feeds the clinical study report (CSR). It argues that a result is evidence to weigh rather than a binary verdict, using the COVID anticoagulation multiplatform trial and the aducanumab Phase III program as cases, and says to report effect sizes and confidence intervals against the minimum clinically important difference. It then covers site closeout and investigational product (IP) accounting, a checklist for closing out computerized systems and external data flows, results posting to ClinicalTrials.gov under the Food and Drug Administration Amendments Act (FDAAA 801), data sharing, and post hoc analyses. It classifies trial failures as efficacy, safety or operational, lists the costs of delay, and describes artificial intelligence (AI) as a way to reduce reconciliation work under governance. A reader can plan closeout and write up a negative or discordant result.
