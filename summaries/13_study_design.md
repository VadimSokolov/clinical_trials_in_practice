This chapter asks which design choices, made before the first patient is enrolled, let an observed effect be credited to the intervention. It covers randomization (simple, block, stratified), levels of blinding, placebo, active, historical and external controls, digital twins, Bayesian borrowing, parallel, crossover, factorial, cluster and stepped wedge designs, and the four inputs to sample size. It then treats master protocols (basket, umbrella and platform trials), the arithmetic of a shared control arm, and case studies: REMAP-CAP, COVID-19 anticoagulation platforms, SNAP, PANTHER, Healey ALS and EPAD. It closes with the statistical analysis plan, the estimands addendum of the International Council for Harmonisation (ICH), the protocol (ICH E6(R3), SPIRIT 2025, ICH M11) and protocol amendments under Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and European Union rules, with Tufts CSDD benchmarks. A reader can compare design types, estimate the enrollment a shared control arm saves, and plan protocol amendments.
