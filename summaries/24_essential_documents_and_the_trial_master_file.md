This chapter answers what documentation shows that a trial was conducted properly, where each document is kept, and how long it is retained. It defines essential documents under International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) E6(R2) and the essential records of ICH E6(R3), and separates the sponsor's trial master file (TMF) from the investigator site file (ISF) and the electronic site file (eISF). It tabulates the critical documents inspectors examine (protocol, Investigator's Brochure, informed consent forms, U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Form 1572, delegation log, ethics approvals, financial disclosure, laboratory certifications, monitoring reports, drug accountability) and states the investigator financial disclosure rules of 21 CFR Part 54. It applies the ALCOA, ALCOA+ and ALCOA++ record quality principles, and describes TMF filing targets, the TMF Reference Model and TMF health metrics. It closes with archiving and retention in the United States, the European Union and ICH E6(R3). A reader can organize and review a TMF and work out which retention period applies.
